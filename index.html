Point the forge at a real churn dataset

This is the sovereign pattern forge run on 7,043 real telecom customers (IBM's public Telco Customer Churn set). It proposes simple churn patterns from a training split, keeps only the ones that still predict a held-out split of customers it never saw, breeds compound rules, and mints the winner as a tiny owned model. The alternative to renting an LLM to read your churn data — re-derived live in your browser from the gated kernel.

The patterns that actually predict churn

Every pattern the forge proposed, graded on the held-out third of customers (balanced accuracy — never an LLM judge). keep = beat the bar and generalised; drop = failed on customers it never saw.

Does breeding compound rules help here?

Breeding ANDs patterns together across generations — selecting on train, judged on held-out. On this real data we report what actually happened, not what we hoped.

Mint it — own the churn model

The validated classifier is a tiny, owned model. Minting is gated by fallforgemint's own rule — a node mints only on a certified BEATS-the-base receipt — and the economics are its own own-vs-rent calculator, costed against renting an LLM to score every customer.

The sealed claims

Pre-registered in predictions.json before measurement; checked live against the kernel on the real data now. The same checks run on GitHub's own runner in CI.

    How this replaces RAG-on-your-CSV

    The usual way to "ask AI about your churn" is to embed every row and rent an LLM to read them back — a per-call bill, your customer data leaving the building, and an answer you cannot re-check. The forge does the opposite: it finds the handful of rules that genuinely predict churn on held-out customers, proves each one survives data it never saw, and hands you a tiny owned model (20 bytes here) you run yourself for nothing. Bring your own CSV (same 21-column shape) and the same pipeline runs on it.
